Break numbers into friendly parts.

For 46 + 8, add 4 to reach 50, then add the remaining 4. The answer is 54. For 37 + 58, add 50 first to get 87, then add 8 to get 95.

Choose your starting point

  • Easy: two numbers from 0 to 9, such as 4 + 7 = 11.
  • Medium: one number from 0 to 9 and one from 0 to 99, such as 46 + 8 = 54.
  • Hard: two numbers from 0 to 99, such as 37 + 58 = 95.

When one number has two digits and the other has one, the two-digit number appears first. Try untimed practice to learn the method, then switch to a timed session.
