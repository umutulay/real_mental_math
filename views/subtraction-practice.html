Practice taking numbers apart without negative answers. The larger number always comes first, so every result is zero or positive.

Count up or subtract in parts.

For 46 − 8, subtract 6 to reach 40, then subtract the remaining 2. The answer is 38. For 82 − 37, subtract 30 to reach 52, then subtract 7 to get 45.

Another method is to count up from 37 to 82: 3 to reach 40, 40 to reach 80, and 2 to reach 82. Add the jumps: 3 + 40 + 2 = 45.

Three levels, no negative results

  • Easy: two numbers from 0 to 9, such as 8 − 3 = 5.
  • Medium: one number from 0 to 9 and one from 0 to 99, such as 46 − 8 = 38.
  • Hard: two numbers from 0 to 99, such as 82 − 37 = 45.

Equal numbers can appear and give an answer of zero. Start untimed if you want to work through each step.
