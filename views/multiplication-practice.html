Split a factor into tens and ones.

For 46 × 8, calculate 40 × 8 = 320 and 6 × 8 = 48. Add them together: 320 + 48 = 368.

For 37 × 58, calculate 37 × 50 = 1850 and 37 × 8 = 296. The total is 2146. You can also use 58 = 60 − 2: 2220 − 74 = 2146.

From facts to larger products

  • Easy: two factors from 0 to 9, such as 4 × 7 = 28.
  • Medium: one factor from 0 to 9 and one from 0 to 99, such as 46 × 8 = 368.
  • Hard: two factors from 0 to 99, such as 37 × 58 = 2146.

If a two-digit factor is paired with a single-digit factor, the two-digit factor appears first. Zero is included in the exercises; any number multiplied by zero gives zero.
