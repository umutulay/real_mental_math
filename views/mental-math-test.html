How the timed test works

  1. Choose a difficulty and a session duration from 25 to 60 seconds.
  2. Start the test. The countdown begins when the first question appears.
  3. Type an answer. Correct answers advance automatically; incorrect answers stay editable.
  4. When time runs out, review your correct-answer count, best streak, and session time.

What to expect

The test mixes addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Subtraction never produces a negative result, and division has whole-number answers. Addition and multiplication put the two-digit number first when paired with a single digit.

Easy uses small numbers; medium introduces larger numbers; hard uses numbers up to 99 for addition, subtraction, and multiplication. Division answer ranges are 1–9, 1–30, and 1–99 respectively.

Make your comparisons fair

Compare your correct-answer count using the same difficulty and duration. The personal best is saved in this browser for matching settings. Incorrect edits are not counted as separate attempts, so accuracy reflects resolved questions rather than every number typed.
